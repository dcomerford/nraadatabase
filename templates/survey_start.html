This is an independent MCSI research project. It is not run by or affiliated with the NRAA.

Help us understand how shooters across disciplines compare. You'll be shown 10 random matches from past Kings & Queens shoots. Each shows a real score from each discipline for that same match.

Drag the rows into the order you think they should rank — 1 = best, down to 5 = least best. You'll only see discipline, distance and score — not who shot it.
